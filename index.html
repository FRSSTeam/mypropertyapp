A Smarter Way to Manage Your Home

The House Management App is a modern, all‑in‑one platform designed to simplify how homeowners, renters, and landlords organise their homes. From financial tracking to document storage and legally compliant contract generation, the app brings every essential part of home management into one secure, intuitive space.

Instead of juggling bills, emails, folders, and scattered apps, users gain a single hub for expenses, warranties, insurance policies, rental agreements, maintenance requests, and more. With built‑in AI assistance, automated reminders, and transparent communication tools, the House Management App removes friction, reduces risk, and gives every household complete clarity and control.

The MyProperty 4‑Step SPEED Setup

Get started in minutes — a simple, guided onboarding designed for every type of household.

1. Create Your Property Profile

Add your home or rental property details — address, type, occupants, and key information. This becomes your central hub for everything that follows.

2. Upload Documents & Contracts

Store warranties, receipts, insurance policies, rental agreements, and manuals. The app automatically organises and tags them for quick access.

3. Set Up Payments & Reminders

Connect bills, rent, subscriptions, and household expenses. Smart reminders ensure you never miss a payment or renewal again.

4. Invite Tenants or Co‑Owners

Share access securely with tenants, partners, or family members. Control permissions, track activity, and maintain a full audit trail.
