What it does

Everything property,
in one place.

01
Digital Contracts & E-Signatures
Generate legally compliant tenancy agreements for Ireland (RTB) and the UK (AST). Both parties e-sign and instantly receive a sealed PDF copy.
02
Maintenance Management
Log issues, track progress from open to resolved, set reminders. Landlords and tenants stay aligned without the constant back-and-forth.
03
Property Key System
Every property gets a unique MP-XXXXXX code. Tenants enter it once to link themselves — no complex invite flows, no friction.
04
Secure Document Storage
A personal file vault for each user. Organise contracts, receipts, photos, and legal documents — all searchable, all in one secure space.
05
Income & Analytics
A live income dashboard for landlords. See monthly rental income, occupancy rates, and maintenance costs across your entire portfolio at a glance.
06
Three Tailored Experiences
One app, three distinct roles. Landlords, tenants, and owner-occupiers each get a bespoke interface built exactly for their needs — nothing more, nothing less.
