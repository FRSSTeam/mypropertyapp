What it does

Everything property,
in one place.

From the first contract to the last maintenance ticket — MyProperty keeps every part of a tenancy organised, compliant and effortless.

Digital contracts & e-signatures
Generate legally compliant tenancy agreements for Ireland (RTB) and the UK (AST). Both parties e-sign and instantly receive a sealed PDF copy.
Maintenance management
Log issues, track progress from open to resolved, and set reminders. Landlords and tenants stay aligned without the constant back-and-forth.
Property key system
Every property gets a unique MP-XXXXXX code. Tenants enter it once to link themselves — no complex invite flows, no friction.
Secure document storage
A personal file vault for each user. Organise contracts, receipts, photos and legal documents — all searchable, all in one secure space.
Income & analytics
A live finance dashboard for landlords. See monthly rental income, occupancy and maintenance costs across your whole portfolio at a glance.
Three tailored experiences
One app, three distinct roles. Landlords, tenants and owner-occupiers each get an interface built exactly for their needs — nothing more, nothing less.
